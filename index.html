Console 02 / Pipeline

What happens after you hit predict

Your inputs travel the same route every listing takes through the trained pipeline.

Stop 1

Validation

Pydantic checks ranges — latitude between −90 and 90, availability between 0 and 365 — before anything reaches the model.

Stop 2

Impute & encode

Numeric gaps are filled with the median, categoricals with the most frequent value, then borough and neighbourhood are one-hot encoded.

Stop 3

Scale & vote

Numeric features are standardized, then 300 decision trees each cast a vote across the three room-type classes.
